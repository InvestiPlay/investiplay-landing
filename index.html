Made by students, for students.

InvestiPlay was founded by two high school students who recognized a gap in traditional education. They built the platform they never had, one that teaches real financial skills through real experience.

What is InvestiPlay

A whole economy in one app, and every part of it is earned.

Students move through a ten-level curriculum of short lessons taught by Jeff, our mascot and AI tutor. Every lesson they master unlocks something real to do with it: the stock market, The Lab, their own micro-business, the bank.

Learn first, then playThe market, The Lab and the business sim stay locked until the matching lesson is done. Mastery is the only key.
Virtual coins, real consequencesStudents earn InvestiCoins for mastery and spend them on trades, rent and payroll. Never real money.
Adaptive by designA benchmark places each student, and question difficulty adjusts to how they answer, so nobody is bored or lost.

01 — MISSIONS

Lessons that feel like a conversation, not a textbook.

Jeff teaches every lesson in short, interactive exchanges with scenarios, reflections and adaptive practice. Finish a mission, earn XP and coins, and unlock the next piece of the economy.

AI tutorAdaptive difficultyStreaks & XP
02 — LIVE MARKET

Trade real tickers at real prices, with zero real risk.

Students build a portfolio from actual market data, track P&L over weeks, and learn what volatility feels like before it ever costs them anything.

Real last-close pricesPortfolio P&LClass leaderboard
03 — THE LAB

Fill out the paperwork adulthood will hand them.

W-4s, I-9s, direct-deposit forms and W-2s, completed in a safe sandbox with instant checking. The first time they see these forms should not be their first day of work.

Real federal formsInstant feedbackCoin rewards
04 — MICRO-BUSINESS

Run a company through months of real decisions.

Hire, price, market and survive random events across a weekly loop with procedural content and a class rank ladder. Written reflections turn every decision into a lesson.

Weekly loopRandom eventsWritten reflections
05 — INVESTIBANK

Four floors of banking, from savings to Wall Street.

Watch compound interest work in The Vault, borrow and build credit at the Lending Desk, trade bonds, then unlock the Executive Floor with persistent career simulations in venture capital, private equity, wealth management and investment banking.

Compound interestCredit scoreCareer sims
06 — TEACHER DASHBOARD

See what the class actually understands, while you can still act on it.

Run live checkpoints mid-lecture and watch the answer distribution on your screen. A class mastery view surfaces the weakest concepts before the test, and assignments push by block with a due date.

Live checkpointsClass mastery viewAssign by block

Built by students, for students

Three students who built the tool they wished they'd had.

InvestiPlay began in our own high school classrooms, where we saw how little students are taught about managing money. We set out to close that gap by designing the curriculum, building the games and writing the code ourselves. Today we continue to improve the platform every week, guided by feedback from the teachers and students who use it.

The pilot program

Fifteen minutes of your block. A semester of better-prepared students.

Our pilot program drops into an existing course without replacing the teacher. Students do a five-minute primer before class, answer live checkpoints during it, and practice after. You get data you don't have today.

  • Free for pilot classrooms. No contracts, no procurement. We set up your class in under a week.
  • Mapped to your course. We align lessons and checkpoints to your existing chapters and state standards.
  • Founders on call. Weekly check-ins with Eduardo and Devon, and fixes shipped within days, not semesters.
  • Data you keep. Per-concept mastery for every student, exportable at the end of the pilot.
